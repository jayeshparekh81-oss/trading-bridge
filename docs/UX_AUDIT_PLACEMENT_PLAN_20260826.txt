================================================================================
TRADETRI — FULL-SITE UX AUDIT + PLACEMENT PLAN
================================================================================
Date    : 2026-08-26
Method  : READ-ONLY audit of the codebase at main @ c0c02b8 (deployed tip).
          54 routes, ~60 customer-facing surfaces. Nothing was changed.
Pattern : Built against the researched market leaders (Tradetron / StrykeX),
          not invented.

THE PROBLEM RESTATED: pieces were built over weeks and scattered. The founder,
an admin who knows the product, logged in and could not find or reach most of it.

THE HEADLINE FINDING: the marketplace journey is REAL end to end. There are no
mocks, no ComingSoon components and no hardcoded datasets anywhere in
components/marketplace/. Every page hits a live endpoint. The problem is NOT
that things are fake — it is that they are UNREACHABLE or UNNAMED.

================================================================================
THE FIVE FINDINGS THAT EXPLAIN "I COULDN'T FIND IT"
================================================================================
1. SUBSCRIBE ENDS IN A TOAST. No redirect, no next step. The customer is left on
   the listing page with no path forward.
     subscribe-button.tsx:106  toast.success("Subscribed - happy trading!")

2. THE DEPLOY CONFIG IS BEHIND AN 11px GREY "Settings" TEXT BUTTON, inside a
   collapsed row. The industry makes this an explicit STEP with a button.
     marketplace/me/page.tsx:302-311  onClick={() => setOpen(v => !v)}
     mounts only when `configurable && open`

3. /marketplace/me IS NOT IN THE SIDEBAR. It is reachable only by first landing
   on /marketplace and clicking through. (Correction to an earlier draft of this
   audit: it is NOT a true orphan — marketplace/page.tsx:92 and :99 link to it.)

4. MARKETPLACE IS ENTIRELY ABSENT FROM MOBILE NAVIGATION — both the drawer and
   the bottom bar. On a phone the whole subscribe journey is unreachable by
   clicking. (grep "marketplace" in mobile-drawer.tsx and mobile-nav.tsx = 0)

5. ClosePositionButton IS AN ORPHAN. No page mounts it, so the one control in
   the subscriber surface that ACTS cannot be clicked by any user.
     grep -rn "ClosePositionButton" returns only its definition + its test

the exit order. We have the drift flip (auto-detect + switch to MANUAL) and the

CAVEAT BEFORE STEP 3 — READ THIS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Direction and Vehicle inside the settings panel are PREVIEW-ONLY:
  - Direction is deliberately NOT sent to the backend (the schema is
    extra="forbid" and would 422)
  - Vehicle is a hardcoded useState("futures") placeholder, labelled
    "Strategy ke instrument se aayega - abhi placeholder"
  - lots_override / execution_mode / is_paper ARE sent, but the backend answers
    applied:false and the UI honestly shows a "Preview" banner

Promoting that panel to a headline "DEPLOY" step makes those two fields look
authoritative when they are not. EITHER wire them to the backend first, OR ship
the Deploy step with those two clearly marked "coming soon". Dressing a preview
as a commitment is the opposite of the discipline held everywhere else in this
product.
