HONEST ASSESSMENT
==============================================================================

  Is Jan-2018 -> Jul-2021 buildable as registered? NO — not as a CANSLIM test.
  The price side came out clean: Dhan serves 2015 onward, the series is
  genuinely back-adjusted (BPCL's 1:1 bonus moves -2.6%, its 1:2 moves +0.6%,
  RELIANCE's 1:1 moves -0.6%, where a raw series would show -50/-33/-50%), no
  >=25% cliff in six years matches a corp action, and the overlap with
  Round-1's habitat shows zero persistent steps. 173 symbols survive with no
  audit exclusions. If this were a price-only strategy it would be a GO.

  The C-gate is what fails, and the damage is concentrated and measurable:
  the first five quarters of the window run at 9-15% eligibility. Kitna skew?
  15 of the window's 42 months are effectively BANK-ONLY — banks 88-94%
  eligible, everything else 0-3%, NBFCs and insurers at zero. A backtest run
  over Jan-2018 -> Jul-2021 as registered would spend its first 15 months
  trading a 17-name bank universe and its remaining 27 months trading 173
  names, and would report the average as one number. That is not a weaker
  CANSLIM test; it is two different strategies concatenated, with the regime
  break placed exactly where the data changes rather than where the market did.

  THE SINGLE BIGGEST REMAINING RISK is that this skew is invisible in every
  aggregate M6c would naturally compute. Trade counts, win rates and
  expectancy all average across the boundary silently; nothing in a results
  table announces that 2018's trades could only have come from banks. The
  mitigation is structural, not statistical: either start the sealed window at
  2019-Q2 (73%+ coverage, but only ~2.25 years and dominated by the COVID
  crash and recovery), or keep Jan-2018 and pre-register that 2018-Q1..2019-Q1
  results are reported SEPARATELY and never pooled with the rest. Insurers
  should be dropped from the Round-2 universe outright — they are at 0% for
  every quarter of the window and can contribute nothing but noise.
