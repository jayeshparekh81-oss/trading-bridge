WHAT WAS ACTUALLY LEARNED
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * THE ENTRY RULES HAVE NO MEASURABLE EDGE.
    An earlier draft of this note claimed the entry added a constant
    0.54-0.74 R over a same-holding-period random entry. THAT CLAIM WAS
    AN ARTEFACT of a broken null and is WITHDRAWN. Against the corrected
    null, 7 of the 9 variants run have NEGATIVE edge. The headline
    configuration (M2 config A) sits 0.2834 R BELOW the null mean.
    The two positive readings — config B +0.1025 R and M3 EMA150
    +0.0414 R — carry p = 0.204 and p = 0.254, i.e. noise.
    Almost all the variation in headline results is HOLDING PERIOD.

  * Exit speed IS a real lever, and it points toward SLOWER. Return rose
    monotonically across EMA 20 -> 50 -> 150 -> 220 with no inflection.
    N=220 is the best rung and nothing rewards moving off it.

  * A PROFIT-CONDITIONAL trail is WORSE than unconditional exit speed at
    the same holding period, because it selectively targets the largest
    winners. SCALE-10x held longer than EMA150 and returned less.

  * Portfolio drawdown was NOT controllable by any exit rule tested. The
    drawdown column showed no direction in either Module 3 or Module 4.
    It is a market event, not an exit-rule variable.

ADVERSARIAL AUDIT — EVERY DEFECT FOUND WAS FLATTERING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A 360-degree correctness audit was run over Modules 2/3/4 AFTER the
  park (scripts/club1pct_audit.py). It attacked flattering and damaging
  errors with equal effort. It found EIGHT defects. EVERY ONE of them
  pointed the same way — flattering. NONE pointed the other way.

  1. VERDICT-DECIDING. The random-entry null passed a ONE-SHARE price as
     the trade notional, so the flat Rs 15.93 DP sell fee was charged
     against a ~Rs 493 median notional while the strategy paid it on a
     ~Rs 1.47 lakh position — a 1,096x handicap, 0.7053 R per trade.
     FIXED and re-measured. Consequence below.
  2. The null drew from `ema220 finite`, true from a symbol's first bar,
     so 12.0% of the pool (56,212 of 468,598 cells) was pre-warmup and
     untradeable. FIXED: it now draws from the strategy's `eligible` flag.
  3. The null excludes actual signal days, making it a 'random NON-signal
     entry'. LEFT IN PLACE by design, but MEASURED: the tilt is at most
     0.098 R and mostly noise-level. It was NOT materially distorting the
     comparison.
  4. Position budget basis is post-exit cash + survivors marked at the
     prior close, which is not equity[d-1]; one trade of 125 breached the
     10% cap, at 10.0149%. Immaterial (0.015pp).
  5. Final equity marks open positions with no exit costs, while the
     ledger books Rs 5,633 of hypothetical sell costs. CAGR flattered by
     about 0.10%.
  6. The p-value is ONE-SIDED. This was never stated in any report until
     now. It is stated here: p = fraction of null iterations whose mean
     equals or exceeds the strategy's expectancy. One-sided.
  7. Module 3 recomputed EMA on the pivoted matrix, which differs for the
     five symbols with data gaps (max 0.96% relative, PATANJALI). No
     effect at N=220. Previously reported.
  8. `shares <= 0` was counted as a 'cash skip' when it actually means the
     share price exceeds 10% of equity. Mislabels skip counts only.

  WHAT PASSED, verified rather than assumed: no look-ahead at any use
  site (252-max exclusive of today, entries at the NEXT open, exits
  decided on the signal close, ranking from signal-day data); cash + market
  value reconstructed independently from the ledger matches the stored
  equity curve to Rs 0.000000 across all 2,879 sessions; the ledger
  reconciles to final equity with residual -Rs 0.00; costs charged and
  independently recomputed on BOTH sides of every trade; slippage adverse
  on both sides; no trade on a missing session; no overlapping positions;
  cash never negative.

  -Rs 4,04,826 = -25.36%. Of that, only Rs 24,243 (6.0%) was realised

  Read it honestly, both halves. Through the crash itself the book fell
  22.9% against the index's 38.4% — it materially OUTPERFORMED, and the

  between 2020-03-23 and 2020-05-27 NIFTY rose about 22% while the
  strategy fell a further 3%. Nine positions exited on the EMA220 leg
  between 11 and 24 March — at the bottom — and the book re-entered in
  April into HINDUNILVR, ALKEM and BHARTIARTL, each of which promptly lost
  about 15%. The depth is the market. The LAG is the strategy.
