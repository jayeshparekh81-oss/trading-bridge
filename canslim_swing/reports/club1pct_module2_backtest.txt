REGISTERED SPEC
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  ENTRY (all true on the close; fill at the NEXT session's OPEN)
    F1  SMA150 > EMA220
    F2  close > SMA50
    F3  SMA50 > SMA150
    F4  close > 1.25 * 52-week low (min of LOW over prior 252 sessions)
    F5  >=1 session in the last 90 where LOW < EMA220
    BRK close > max CLOSE of the prior 252 sessions, excluding today
    No second entry into a symbol already held.
  CONFIG A = the above.   CONFIG B = identical with F5 REMOVED.
    B was registered in advance, before any result existed, because the
    Module 1 census showed F5 destroying 67% of signals.

  EXIT (two legs, whichever first; both evaluated on the CLOSE and
        executed at the NEXT session's OPEN — no intraday stop)
    Leg 1  close < EMA220
    Leg 2  close <= 0.85 * entry fill price
    Positions still open on the last panel date are marked to market and
    reported separately from closed trades.

  PORTFOLIO   Rs 10,00,000 start; 10 slots; max 10% of CURRENT equity per
              stock; whole shares, rounded down; compounding. Excess
              signals ranked by 6-month return, highest first. Cash
              short -> signal skipped and recorded (no partial sizing).
  R           = the registered stop distance = 0.15 * entry notional.

  DECLARED EXECUTION CHOICES (stated, not searched)
    - exits processed before entries within a session, so a freed slot
      and its cash are available same-day
    - position budget = 10% of equity marked at the PREVIOUS close;
      costs paid on top of the budget, out of cash
    - equity marking uses forward-filled closes, so a symbol's data gap
      cannot blank the curve

11. RANDOM-ENTRY NULL — the regime thermometer
==============================================================================
   Design reused from scripts/m5_select_nulls.random_entry_null: matched
   trade count, holding periods resampled from the strategy's own
   distribution, random symbol on random eligible NON-signal days,
   identical costs and slippage, 500 iterations, seed 20260816.
   Reimplemented against this module's data structures; one deliberate
   change — the null exits at the OPEN after the sampled hold, to match
   this module's declared exit timing (m5 exited at the close).

   config A:  NULL MEAN +0.4396 R    median +0.2672 R
              p95 +0.9079   p99 +7.4639   skew 5.74
              strategy +0.9814 R   ->   p = 0.0480
   config B:  NULL MEAN +0.5101 R    median +0.3153 R
              p95 +1.0692   p99 +8.4253   skew 6.52
              strategy +1.3208 R   ->   p = 0.0360

   READING: a RANDOM entry into this universe over this period earned
   +0.44 R per trade — about +6.6% net on a median six-month hold, with no
   rule at all. That is what the window was. The null distribution is also
   violently right-skewed (median only +0.27 R, p99 +7.46 R), so the
   strategy's +0.98 R sits barely above the 95th percentile of +0.91.
   Both p-values sit a hair under 0.05 and are decided by a handful of
   extreme tail draws. The gate is recorded as PASS because that is what
   the frozen rule says; the honest reading is that the entry edge is not
   distinguishable from luck at any comfortable margin.

12. BENCHMARK DIAGNOSTIC — added after the verdict, changes nothing
==============================================================================
   Identical window, 2015-01-01 -> 2026-08-14, 2,879 sessions.

   How mid-window listings are handled in the equal-weight benchmarks:
     130 of the 188 symbols exist on day 1; 58 list later (earliest
     2015-04-09, latest 2024-12-18).
     EW BUY & HOLD (primary): Rs 10,00,000 / 188 is allocated per symbol.
       A late lister holds its slice in CASH at 0% return until its first
       session, then buys at that close and holds to the end. No
       rebalancing — winners drift up in weight, which mirrors how
       configs A and B actually behave. The cash held for late listers is
       a genuine drag on this benchmark's return.
     EW REBALANCED DAILY (reference): each day's return is the mean daily
       return of every symbol having both today's and yesterday's close.


                              series  max_dd_pct  longest_dd_days  cagr_pct  total_return_pct
                    NIFTY buy & hold      -38.44              742      9.73             194.1
   Universe EW buy & hold (no rebal)      -35.50              424     18.70             632.3
        Universe EW rebalanced daily      -38.02              709     20.42             766.0
                    Config A (F5 in)      -25.36              540     15.88             454.1
                   Config B (F5 out)      -37.41             1147     16.95             516.3

   CAVEAT, and it is a large one: the 188-symbol universe is TODAY'S F&O
   list frozen in 2026. Holding it from 2015 requires 2015 knowledge of
   the 2026 constituents, so both equal-weight rows are NOT achievable —
   they are an upper bound, not a portfolio anyone could have run. The
   same selection bias flatters configs A and B, which drew their trades
   from that same forward-looking list.

   READING: both configs UNDERPERFORM the equal-weight universe on CAGR
   (A 15.88%, B 16.95%, versus 18.70% held and 20.42% rebalanced). Config
   A does buy a materially better drawdown (-25.4% against -35.5%) and has
   the best return-per-unit-drawdown of anything in the table, so it is
   not strictly dominated. Config B is: worse CAGR, worse drawdown, and a
   drawdown lasting 1,147 days against 424, than simply buying the whole
   universe and doing nothing.

   ON THE GATE: the -15% ceiling was not reachable by any fully-invested
   long-only book in this window. NIFTY itself drew down 38.4%, the
   equal-weight universe 35.5%, both on the same day — 2020-03-23. A
   strategy averaging 80% deployed with a median 133-session hold had no
   mechanism to be flat into that crash. The gate looks mis-set. It
   nevertheless STANDS as registered, and both configs remain FAIL.
