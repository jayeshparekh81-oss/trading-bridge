HONEST ASSESSMENT — does the funnel breathe?

  Across all 987 trading days the scan averages 4.0 candidates/day;
  on the 778 nifty_on days it averages 5.1/day — a STREAM by the
  trickle/stream/flood test. The count is HARD regime-dependent by construction:
  nifty_on is an AND term, so all 209 nifty_off days are exactly zero
  candidates regardless of stock-level quality — and those days still carried
  3.9 symbols passing T+C+base on average, so the market filter alone
  is discarding real setups. Whether that is protection or over-filtering is an
  M4/M5 question, not one this module can answer.
  Even on nifty_on days, 25 (3%) produce zero candidates,
  and 234 of 987 days overall are empty. The binding constraint is visible in
  the funnel above: the trend template is the coarse filter, and the C-gate then
  removes most of what survives it. That ordering is expected for CANSLIM, but it
  means candidate counts are sensitive to the 25% growth threshold — a parameter
  M5 must sweep before anyone reads significance into the trade count.

  ONE THING TO FLAG FOR M5 (observed, not fixed here — the spec is frozen):
  the C-gate requires base EPS > 0 but nothing about base MAGNITUDE, so growth
  off a near-zero base passes easily. 246 of 3,973 candidate-days (6.2%)
  show >500% YoY growth; the median candidate-day is +66% and the 99th is +1790%.
  LAURUSLABS is the clearest case — its quarterly EPS oscillates between roughly
  Rs 0.23 and Rs 1.90, so a one-rupee swing off a depressed base reads as several
  hundred percent. That is arithmetic, not earnings acceleration. A minimum
  absolute base-EPS floor is worth adding to the M5 sweep alongside the growth
  threshold; without it the C-gate systematically favours recovering-from-bad-
  quarter names over steady compounders.

  Caveats inherited and honoured here (from M2b): the C-gate FAILS CLOSED on
  every missing quarter (no interpolation, no carry-forward); each symbol uses
  ONE EPS series for its whole timeline; and the as-of rule keys on announce_ts,
  never on period_end, so late filings (HDFCBANK's 391-day-late Mar-2021
  consolidated result) cannot leak future information into a scan day.
