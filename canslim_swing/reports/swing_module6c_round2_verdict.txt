VERDICT
==============================================================================

  The strategy FAILED its Round-2 sealed test, finishing at the 1st percentile of
  a random-entry null whose own mean was ten times its expectancy.

  REGISTERED WEIGHT: a 2.3-year window carrying 31 trades is ADDITIONAL
  EVIDENCE — ek aur gawah — not a final adjudication, and that was fixed in
  advance rather than chosen after seeing the numbers. This witness says what
  Round-1's said: 99% of the 2,268-config grid was profitable here, so the
  window is tide, not skill; the champion cleared neither the tide nor the null;
  and the trade count is below the registered 60, so the finding is underpowered
  in the direction of caution. Two independent windows have now returned the
  same shape of answer — the build-half edge was regime, and out of sample the
  method has not beaten doing something arbitrary in the same names. That is a
  consistent pair of negative witnesses, not a proof; what would change the
  picture is a window where the grid is genuinely mixed, because only there can
  a champion's rank carry information about method rather than about the market.
